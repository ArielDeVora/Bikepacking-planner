# v0.1.0: first release

A collaborative bikepacking trip planner in the browser: plan a long route together, on a map, in real time.

## Routes and stages
- Draw segments that snap to roads or tracks (BRouter profiles: trekking, gravel, road, mountain bike…), or by train, bus, boat, plane and car.
- Organise them in stages, groups and routes (several versions of the trip side by side); drag to reorder, fuse, split, erase, reverse, put in order.
- Import GPX, KML, TCX and GeoJSON; export GPX or a full JSON backup.
- Elevation profile, distances and climb by travel mode.

## Places
- Places with categories (built-in or your own, each with its own icon and colour), shown as folders you can reorder and drag places between.
- Statuses (idea, must-see, GOATED, skip), votes from everyone, and a crown on places at least three people approve of.
- Search places or coordinates in any common format; OpenStreetMap overlays (water, food, bike shops, campsites…) and base-map places with details and Wikipedia.
- Filter by status or by who added them, or group by country.

## Conditions
- Usual temperature and rain by week of the year (TerraClimate 1991–2020), as map overlays with a slider, and per place or stage.
- Border crossings along the route, travel advice (UK and US), avoid and risk zones.

## Together
- Share a trip and edit it together live, with comments, who-edited-what, and a full history: restore any earlier point or undo one change.
- Settings per browser (sizes, labels, line thickness, theme…), What's new after each update, and a guide.
