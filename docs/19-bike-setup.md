# Bike setup

The bike button in the panel header (or `#bike` in the address) opens a drawing of a gravel bike to
plan racks, bags and cages, and where each piece of gear goes. It is saved **in this browser only**,
one setup per trip: nothing is synced, and the other members don't see it. It is hidden on follow
links.

## The bike

- The drawing is to scale: a 56 cm gravel bike with 700×40 wheels, a 1030 mm wheelbase, 430 mm
  chainstays, 73.5° seat and 71.5° head angles. Bags and bottles are drawn at their real size.
- **Racks, bags and cages** (left): tick what you have. Ticked parts wait on the **Shelf** under the
  bike; drag one onto the bike, or click it, and it snaps into place. Rear panniers and the loads on
  top of the racks go on with their rack, which must be ticked too. Front panniers mount on the
  fork and need no rack.
- "On the front/rear rack" is a **Bag** or an **Open load** (a load strapped to a platform such as a
  pizza rack). The frame bag is **Half** or **Full**.
- Bottle cages: on the down tube, on the seat tube, under the down tube, and a pair on the fork.
- To take a part off, drag its card's title back to the shelf, or click its ×. Its gear goes back
  to the list, and comes back with it when it goes on again.
- Warnings: a full frame bag with cages inside the frame, a seat pack with a load on the rear rack,
  front panniers with fork cages.

## Gear

- **My gear** lists the checklist's items that are for you or that you bought (with **Include shared
  gear**, the shared items too), with their number, weight, what's on the bike and a progress bar.
- Drag an item onto a bag in the drawing or onto its card (each card is numbered like the drawing).
  Panniers and fork cages have a **Left** and a **Right** list; the pannier seen in the drawing is the
  right one. Items can be dragged from bag to bag; × sends one back to the list.
- **The bag itself**: drag the item that is the bag, rack or cage (e.g. "Ortlieb Back-Roller") onto
  the dashed slot at the top of its card. Its weight becomes the part's weight, split between a
  pair (a pair at 1.9 kg, or one pannier ×2 at 950 g, counts 950 g per bag), and it leaves the list.
  Without one, a weight can be typed next to the part on the left (for one bag of a pair).
- **On me**: what you wear or carry on your back (helmet, jacket, hydration pack). It counts in the
  climbing total, not on the bike.

## Weights

- One row per bag: the bag itself, its gear, the total, and a bar. Then the racks.
- Where the weight sits (rear, frame and bars, front), left against right, and **Luggage on the
  wheels**: how much presses on each wheel, worked out from where each bag sits between the axles.
- Bike + racks, bags and gear = **Loaded bike**; loaded bike + on me + you = **Everything that
  climbs**. The bike's and your weights are typed in kg.
- Bags and racks count only once they have a weight. Gear weighs its weight × quantity; items
  without a weight count as 0 g and are flagged.
